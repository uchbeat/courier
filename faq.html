How do I track my shipment?

You can track your package in real-time by entering your unique tracking code (such as GEO123456789) on our Track Shipment page or directly in the search bar on our homepage hero section.

How are shipping costs calculated?

Freight charges are calculated based on chargeable weight, which is the greater of actual weight or dimensional (volumetric) weight (L × W × H / 5000), along with destination distance, insurance options, and speed tier (Economy, Express, or Priority). Use our Shipping Calculator for an immediate estimate.

What should I do if my tracking status says "Customs Delay"?

Customs holds usually occur when destination authorities require additional commercial invoices, tax declarations, or package inspection. If your package experiences a delay, our support team will notify the sender or recipient via email to resolve necessary documentation requirements.

Which items are restricted or hazardous for air transport?

Restricted items include lithium-ion batteries exceeding regulation thresholds, flammable liquids, pressurized gases, explosives, weapons, perishable food products without specialized cold-chain clearance, and currency. Please review your destination country's regulations before booking.

What is the difference between Express and Standard freight?

Priority Air Express provides time-critical flight routing with 1–2 business day arrival windows and direct airport clearance. Standard Freight utilizes consolidated air or ocean routes, offering cost-effective transit typically arriving within 3–8 business days.

What should I do if my package arrives damaged?

If a parcel arrives damaged, please retain all original packaging and photos of the condition. File a formal damage claim through our Contact Page within 48 hours of delivery signature to initiate inspection and insurance reimbursement workflows.

Can I change the delivery address after dispatch?

Address modifications are possible while the package is at an intermediate sorting terminal before dispatching for final doorstep delivery. Address change requests may incur a rerouting fee depending on destination carrier policies.

How do I schedule a pickup at my office or home?

You can schedule a pickup online using our Book a Shipment Wizard. Select your pickup address, weight details, and preferred pickup date to request a courier dispatch.
